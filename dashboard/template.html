Workout Progress

Rules: the session is the next one in the rotation Chest, Shoulders, Back, Legs, Legs + accessories after your last session; a group trained in the last 2 days is skipped. Overdue means one thing: not trained directly for 9 or more days. An overdue main group goes ahead of the rotation, other overdue groups become add-ons (two at most). Bars count working sets in the last 7 days: warm-up sets are left out, an exercise logged without numbers counts as 3 sets, pressing counts half for shoulders and triceps, pulling half for biceps. Weights follow double progression, 8 to 12 reps. See PHILOSOPHY.md in the repo.
